Waste Sorting & Disposal Guidelines

Standard handling protocols, contamination mitigation guidelines, and safe disposal procedures for each material category.

Hazardous Stream

Hazardous Waste

Do not place in regular household trash. Requires specialized municipal hazardous management.
  • Take to a designated municipal hazardous waste collection center or e-waste drop-off point.
  • Keep batteries, electronics, chemicals, and paint cans strictly separate from general bins.
  • Never pour toxic or chemical liquids down drains, sinks, or storm gutters.
  • Store securely in an upright, sealed container until scheduled drop-off.

Organic Stream

Organic Matter

Suitable for municipal green waste collection and aerobic compost enrichment.
  • Dispose in dedicated compost containers or municipal organics/food waste bins.
  • Ensure food scraps are free of plastic wrap, stickers, staples, or synthetic tags.
  • Includes fruit peels, vegetable leftovers, coffee grounds, and garden foliage.
  • Keep organic waste isolated from recyclables to avoid paper and cardboard contamination.

Recyclable Stream

Recyclable Materials

Secondary raw materials intended for mechanical remanufacturing and resource circularity.
  • Rinse plastic bottles, glass jars, and aluminum cans to remove residual liquids or oils.
  • Flatten cardboard packaging and plastic containers to optimize collection volume.
  • Check local municipal recycling rules for accepted rigid polymer grades (#1, #2, #5).
  • Keep paper and cardboard clean and completely dry to maintain fiber strength.

Contamination Prevention Best Practices

Key habits that maintain secondary material quality at processing facilities.

Why does food residue ruin recyclable batches?

Grease and liquids seep into paper and cardboard, breaking down cellulose fibers so they cannot be pulped. Always rinse containers thoroughly.

Why can't batteries go in standard trash?

Lithium and alkaline cells contain heavy chemicals and pose severe fire hazards during garbage truck compaction. Always route them to dedicated e-waste facilities.

What belongs in organic composting?

Raw vegetable peels, fruit rinds, coffee grounds, and yard clippings. Avoid treated wood, plastics, and non-compostable packaging.

How does AI classification improve sorting efficiency?

Instant vision pre-classification eliminates cross-contamination before bins are collected, drastically reducing manual sorting costs and landfill diversion rates.
