Model Specification

MobileNetV2 Waste Classification Pipeline

EcoCycle AI leverages transfer learning on an inverted residual MobileNetV2 backbone pretrained on ImageNet, coupled with a custom dense classification head trained specifically for fine-grained multi-class waste segregation.

Feature Extractor

MobileNetV2 CNN

Utilizes depthwise separable convolutions with inverted residuals and linear bottlenecks for low-latency feature extraction suitable for edge and cloud deployment.

Semantic Pre-Check

Multimodal Groq VLM

A fast vision-language model checks whether images contain actual waste items and extracts natural language item labels (e.g. "lithium battery", "banana peel").

Data Augmentation

Robust Generalization

Trained with real-time augmentation pipelines (rotation, zoom, horizontal flips, channel shifts) to maintain robustness across varied angles and lighting conditions.
